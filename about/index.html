Background

I joined the Human Artificial Intelligence Research Lab as an undergraduate research assistant in 2021. I completed my B.S. in Creative Technology Management at Yonsei’s Underwood International College in 2023, followed by an M.S. in Innovation in 2025, advised by Professor Keeheon Lee.

My master’s research connected document retrieval with knowledge graphs for biomedical question answering. My subsequent projects have covered multi-agent evidence synthesis, human verification of AI answers, generalization measurement, and sequential editing in scientific search tasks.

Research interests

I’m interested in how language models use evidence, how we measure their learning, and how an agent’s edits affect its remaining options. I use controlled model evaluations and, for human–AI interaction studies, interaction logs and interviews.

I would like to extend this work to learned representations that help agents predict the consequences of their actions. More about these directions ↗
