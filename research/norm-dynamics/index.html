How do shared rules interact with different moral profiles?

A multi-agent sandbox studies how institutional rules and agent composition shape behavior in a shared environment.

Co-authorMulti-agent systems
In this research
The approachWhat we foundScope & limitationsThe next questionPublication
01 / The approach

Turn the question into an experiment.

Agents with illustrative moral profiles interact in a co-living environment with episodic memory and contribution feedback. We compare 15 group compositions under four institutional rule regimes.

02 / What we found

Look at the interaction.

The framework allows composition and shared rules to be varied separately while tracking welfare, equity, communication, and credit-assignment stability.

Scope & limitations

The workshop study is a simulation of artificial agents. Profile labels and simulated outcomes should not be interpreted as validated models of human cultures or evidence for real-world policy.

The question this opens

How can agent simulations help formulate testable questions about coordination and institutions?
