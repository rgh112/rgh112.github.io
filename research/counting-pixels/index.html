How can a simple visual rule help recover data from scientific charts?

Keeheon Lee, Eury Sohn, Kunhee Ryu, and Seongmin Oh

A rule-based axis detector uses pixel counting to locate the structure needed for chart data extraction.

01 / The approach

Study design

The method identifies candidate axis colors, counts pixels, and uses geometric rules to locate chart axes. Axis detection is treated as a component of extracting numerical evidence from chart images.

02 / What we found

Findings

The paper introduces a lightweight rule-based approach to axis detection, motivated by the need to extract and synthesize information from scientific figures.

Scope & limitations

The method addresses axis detection within chart extraction. It does not establish an end-to-end system for interpreting arbitrary scientific figures.

Further questions

How can extracted evidence be connected to its context and used in reliable scientific reasoning?
