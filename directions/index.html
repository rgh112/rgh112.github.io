Research directions

Understand the structure.
Anticipate the change.

I want to develop AI that learns relationships it can use in new situations—and predicts what its actions will make possible.

A research agenda, grounded in the questions my work has opened.
01Represent ↘02Anticipate ↘03Inquire ↗
01Representation & generalization

Learn what carries over.

Which relations must a model preserve to reason in a new situation?

I want to learn representations that capture relationships and changes in the environment. The test is whether those representations support prediction and planning when familiar elements appear in unfamiliar combinations.

How I want to study it

Vary the learned representation and prediction objective, then test unseen combinations of relations and changes in the environment.

What I would measure

Transfer, predictive accuracy, and the quality of downstream decisions.

Where it connects

My work on checkpoint selection asks whether our measurements can detect the progress that precedes a visible change in generalization.

02World models & planning

Anticipate what an action changes.

Can an agent recognize when its next move would close off its goal?

Scientific exploration often proceeds through edits: change a composition, modify a sequence, evaluate the result. I want agents to learn how each edit changes the future options available to them.

How I want to study it

Begin with environments where goal reachability can be checked, then introduce partial observations and changing constraints.

What I would measure

Goal attainment, preservation of feasible paths, constraint violations, and computational cost.

Where it connects

My work on local edit validity makes loss of recoverability observable. It motivates learning to anticipate that loss before acting.

03Information & collaboration

Know what to ask next.

When should an agent observe, consult another agent, or ask a person?

A useful decision may depend on information the agent does not yet have. I want information gathering to be part of planning, with explicit attention to what a new observation could change.

How I want to study it

Control what is visible and remembered; compare additional observations, peer evidence, and expert input under a shared cost budget.

What I would measure

Decision improvement, information cost, correction of errors, and whether people can understand and act on requests.

Where it connects

AutoMETA and my work on clinician trust connect this question to evidence synthesis and human judgment.

Where these questions matter

Scientific discovery.
Autonomous systems.
Human judgment.

Scientific work requires evidence from different sources. Autonomous systems turn predictions into sequences of actions. In both, a person may need to inspect a decision or intervene. I want to use these settings to make learning, reasoning, and planning measurable in the same system.
