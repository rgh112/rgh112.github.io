01 / Grounding

Extracting evidence

My early work focused on extracting data from scientific charts. During my master’s, I worked on biomedical question answering, combining document retrieval with knowledge graphs to connect answers to supporting information.

Chart axis detection ↗
02 / Interaction

Checking evidence

I then worked on how evidence is checked and used. In meta-analysis, we studied agents that review one another’s extractions. In a study of clinician trust, we examined how people inspect the evidence behind an AI answer.

Multi-agent meta-analysis ↗
03 / Action

Evaluating sequences of edits

In scientific search, an agent may change a composition or a sequence through several edits. I study whether an allowed edit can leave the final goal out of reach, and how to identify the step where this happens.

Evaluating sequential edits ↗
04 / Looking ahead

Predicting action outcomes

I’m interested in whether learned representations can help an agent predict the effects of an edit before making it. A related question is when the agent needs another observation or input from a person to decide.
